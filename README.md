# Write-Up App 📋

This app is for your job paperwork. It works on your phone. It works with no signal.

**The app link:** https://johndoegalt.github.io/field-writeup/

---

## 1. Get the app (do this one time)

### 🍎 iPhone

1. Open the link in **Safari**. Safari is the blue compass 🧭.
2. Tap the **Share** button. It is a square with an arrow going up ⬆️.
3. Scroll down. Tap **Add to Home Screen** ➕.
4. Tap **Add**.
5. Go to your Home Screen. Tap the new **Write-Up** icon 📋.

Always open the app with the **icon**. Do not use Safari for your jobs.

### 🤖 Android

1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **Install app**. Or tap the **3 dots** ⋮ at the top, then **Install app**.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Tap the new **Write-Up** icon 📋.

---

## 2. The first time you open it

1. Type **your name**.
2. Type the **office email**.
3. Tap **Save**.

---

## 3. Every job

1. Tap **+ New write-up**.
2. Fill in every box.
3. Take the photos 📷.
4. Ask the customer to sign ✍️. Then you sign.
5. Look at the big button at the bottom.
   - 🟨 **Yellow** = something is missing. Tap it. It shows you what to fix.
   - 🟦 **Blue** = all done! Tap it.
6. Tap **Send to office**. Pick **Mail** ✉️. Tap **Send**.

---

## Good to know 👍

- 📵 No signal? It still works.
- 💾 Your work saves by itself.
- ✉️ No signal when you send? The email goes out when you get signal.
